Corporate Partners

Stand with mountain communities.

Mountain Preservation Foundation works alongside the people who live beneath the world’s great ranges. Corporate partners help that work reach further, starting with flood recovery in Nepal.

Every partnership is shaped around the company. We would rather have a conversation than hand you a rate card.

Ways to Partner

Three ways companies stand with us.

Fund a program

Back a specific piece of the work: relief and rebuilding grants in the Trishuli valley, a school, a clinic, or the livelihood programs that let families stay in the mountains. Partners receive reporting from the field on what their support did.

Recognition from the field

Our team documents the work in person, in October in Nepal and on the ground wherever the Foundation works next. Partners are recognized on this site, in those dispatches, and in our annual report.

Employee giving

Matching programs, workplace campaigns, and team gifts to the Nepal Flood Recovery Fund. We make it simple for your people to take part and report back to them directly.

The Foundation is a Wyoming nonprofit corporation, EIN 42-4666179, with independent governance and separate books from any commercial partner. Corporate gifts carry no goods or services in return.
