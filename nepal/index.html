Why Give Here

This valley is personal.

Our connection to this valley is measured in decades, not news cycles.

Since 2007, Nuwa Estate has been part of the fabric of this place: the Tenzing family farm, the schools and clinics its harvests help support, and the farming families whose livelihoods are rooted here. Our relationship with Nuwa brought us to this valley long before the flood.

But today, the need is here.

Nepal Flood Recovery Fund

Our response begins with the people already there.

On August 26, flash floods tore through communities along the Trishuli River in Rasuwa and Nuwakot, destroying homes, roads, farmland and critical infrastructure.

Through long-standing partners on the ground, the Nepal Flood Recovery Fund supports immediate relief and rebuilding in affected communities, putting resources directly into the hands of organizations and people who know these villages, know these families, and understand what is needed now.

Where Your Gift Goes

Planet 5 covers the Foundation’s operating costs, and our team funds its base travel personally.

Donations to the Nepal Flood Recovery Fund are reserved for relief and rebuilding grants in the affected communities and direct field costs associated with documenting the recovery work. Every dollar is accounted for publicly.

The goal is simple: keep as much of your gift as possible where it belongs, in the valley, helping people recover and rebuild.

We welcome all gift sizes; all gifts go to the Nepal Flood Recovery Fund. Mountain Preservation Foundation is a Wyoming nonprofit corporation (EIN 42-4666179). Our federal 501(c)(3) application is in progress; once granted, exempt status applies retroactively, covering gifts made today. Gifts support rebuilding grants through our on-ground partners and the direct field costs of the October documentation mission; the fund is used to help the valley recover and rebuild, and all uses are documented publicly.

October

In October, we will be there ourselves.

Our team will travel into the affected areas alongside Nuwa Estate, the Nepal Green Tara Foundation and other local partners. We’ll see the first stages of recovery firsthand, meet with families and local businesses, document what has been lost and what is being rebuilt, and listen to what these communities need next.

We’ll bring those stories home: the people, the photographs, the progress and the work still ahead.
