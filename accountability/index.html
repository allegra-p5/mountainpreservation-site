How Our Model Works

A different way to fund preservation.

Mountain Preservation Foundation was designed so that philanthropy can remain focused on the work.

Planet 5 has committed a portion of revenue from its Guardian Program to help support the Foundation’s operating structure. That commitment is made under a written agreement, and our commercial and philanthropic activities remain separate, with independent governance and accounting. The Guardian Program is a Planet 5 product; you can read about it on planet5media.com.

Donors to the Foundation are giving to the Foundation alone. Gifts carry no goods, services, or membership benefits. Designated gifts, such as the Nepal Flood Recovery Fund, are reserved for the designated program and its direct field costs.

The purpose is simple: keep more philanthropic capital where it belongs, in the mountains.

Reporting

As we grow, our reporting grows with us.

The Foundation was incorporated in Wyoming on August 24, 2026. Our first program is the Nepal Flood Recovery Fund, and our first report will be the accounting of that fund: every grant, with photographs and receipts from the October field mission, published on this site.

From there: annual reports, IRS Form 990 filings, and audited financial statements, published every year. Outcomes will be reported against stated goals, from habitat protected and livelihoods supported to schools, health programs and community initiatives.
