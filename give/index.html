Urgent · Trishuli Valley, Nepal

Nepal Flood Recovery Fund

On August 26 a glacial collapse sent a wall of water down the Trishuli, through the villages where our work began. This fund makes rebuilding grants through partners who have worked in the valley for decades, and it pays the direct costs of our October field mission. Every dollar is publicly accounted for.
